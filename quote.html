QUOTE FAQS

Common questions about getting a quote

How long does it take to get a quote?

We typically respond to quote requests within 24 hours with an initial proposal. For complex events, we may require up to 48 hours to prepare a comprehensive quote.

Is there any cost to get a quote?

No, our quoting service is completely free with no obligation. We only charge for services once you've approved a quote and signed our service agreement.

What information do I need to provide?

The more details you can provide about your event (date, location, expected attendees, services needed), the more accurate our quote will be. However, we can provide preliminary estimates with basic information.

Can I get a quote for multiple event options?

Absolutely! We're happy to provide comparative quotes for different venues, service packages, or event formats to help you make the best decision.

How long is a quote valid for?

Our quotes are typically valid for 14 days, after which we may need to review pricing based on vendor availability and other factors.
